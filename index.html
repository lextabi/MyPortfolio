About

A production-focused engineer with a DevOps and automation mindset.

I'm a Manila-based Senior DevOps Engineer at Accenture with 8+ years of experience keeping enterprise applications healthy in production. I handle the full path from L2 incident triage, database queries, and message-flow troubleshooting to L3 releases, fix packs, configuration changes, and disaster-recovery readiness.
